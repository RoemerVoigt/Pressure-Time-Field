## 📖 Abstract

This repository presents the theoretical foundation of the **Pressure–Time Field (PTF)** model, a classical field framework coupling pressure gradients and temporal dynamics as a source of motion and energy transfer.  
Version 1.3 formalizes the Lagrangian, derives the Euler–Lagrange field equation, and constructs the energy–momentum tensor linking PTF to general relativity.
